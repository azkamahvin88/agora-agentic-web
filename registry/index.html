Add your shop

  1. Copy the shop template and publish it on your own static site (GitHub Pages works).
  2. Fill in your catalog, prices, and delivery details in the template's network file.
  3. Make sure agent-manifest.json is reachable at your site root.
  4. Open a pull request on the Agora repo adding one entry to registry.json (name, manifest URL, capabilities).

No account, fee, or approval is needed to publish your own storefront — the registry is only a discovery list. Your shop works with or without being listed here.
